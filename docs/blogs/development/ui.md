**The UI Process**

When I went into creating the app, something I wanted to perfect was the UI of the app. I had just come from a CS class in school that focused on pleasing customers and UI/UX design: I thought that was the hardest part of software design. I was wrong by a lot. Design in the real world is much differnt than in a controlled enviorment. Out in the open, there are much more influencing factors. For example, vibrant design, the trademark of vibe-coded designs, aren't actually popular. Furthermore, the app has to be accessible for all sorts of users. 

I come from a website development and designing background, there were lots of things that were new to me in the world of iOS app design. There are some limitations, and some advantages of both worlds. MI could use my CSS skills and write the app in React Native, having just learned the basics of React through the Vite build tool. However, writing the styles in CSS for an iOS app would mean losing things such as immersive designs, interactive widgets and dynamic island actions. 

Therefore, the app was designed using SwiftUI.
