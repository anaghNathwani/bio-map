## Notes

- Instagram doesn't make links in captions or comments tappable. Put the link in your bio (and say "link in bio" in the post if you like), or use a Link sticker on a Story.
- The phone screens are mockups drawn for this ad (generic icons, not captures of Apple's apps), so they show the flow, not exact pixels.
- The invite link only works while the TestFlight group is open and its build is approved. The tester limit on the public link is the rollout throttle: raise it in App Store Connect as you go.
