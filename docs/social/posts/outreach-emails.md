# Outreach emails

Send individually, one at a time, to a real person. Look up something specific about them first. Keep it short, ask for one thing,
follow up once after a week, then stop. Replace the [brackets].

## Local Audubon chapter / bird club

**Subject:** Free bird ID app built for birders: could I get your feedback?

Hi [name],

I saw [something specific: a recent walk, newsletter item]. I'm building BioMap, a free citizen science project: a bird photo or a recorded call gets an ID suggestion on your phone, and other birders can confirm or correct it. It says when it isn't confident instead of guessing.

The iOS app is coming soon, and a live map works in any browser: https://getbiomap.web.app/map

Would you be open to trying it, or would a five-minute demo at a meeting be useful? I'd value your members' honest feedback most, especially on which birds it gets wrong.

Thanks,
[your name]
