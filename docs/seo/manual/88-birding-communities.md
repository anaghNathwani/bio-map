# eBird, Cornell and BirdNET communities

**Why it helps:** The people who know birds and bird-sound tech are the best early testers and the most credible mentions.

**Who does it:** you (needs your account or your judgement; it can't be done from the repo).

## Steps

1. **First, check the BirdNET license.** BirdNET's model is published under terms with attribution (and, as of my knowledge, non-commercial) conditions. Confirm the current license in the BirdNET-Analyzer repository and make sure the app credits Cornell Lab of Ornithology. Do this before promoting widely.
2. Then join the BirdNET discussion boards or GitHub Discussions and share the project where showcase posts are welcome.
3. Say clearly that BioMap uses BirdNET as released and isn't affiliated with Cornell unless that's true.

**Done when:** the license/credit check is done and you've posted or decided not to.
