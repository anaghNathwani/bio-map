# Product Hunt

**Why it helps:** Free launch platform; the page itself is a backlink and can drive a burst of visitors.

**Who does it:** you (needs your account or your judgement; it can't be done from the repo).

## Steps

1. Wait until the iOS app is actually installable; Product Hunt expects a product people can use.
2. Prepare: tagline, 3-5 screenshots, a 60-second demo video, the maker's first comment.
3. Schedule the launch for 12:01am Pacific on a Tuesday-Thursday.

**Done when:** the launch is scheduled or live.
