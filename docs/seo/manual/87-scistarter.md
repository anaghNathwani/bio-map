# SciStarter project listing

**Why it helps:** SciStarter is a large citizen-science project directory; its audience is people looking for projects to join.

**Who does it:** you (needs your account or your judgement; it can't be done from the repo).

## Steps

1. Go to https://scistarter.org and create an account, then use *Add a project*.
2. Have ready: a description, who it's for, what participants do, and how the data is used (link `/citizen-science`).
3. Be candid that it is an early project. Their reviewers may ask about a research or education goal.

**Done when:** the project is submitted or live.
