# Audubon chapters, birding clubs and schools

**Why it helps:** Local clubs have newsletters and meetings, and their websites give high-quality links.

**Who does it:** you (needs your account or your judgement; it can't be done from the repo).

## Steps

1. Find your local Audubon chapter, bird club and any university ornithology or environmental club.
2. Send a short, personal email offering a demo and asking for feedback. Template:

> Hi <name>, I built BirdMap, a free citizen science project where bird photos and calls are identified on your phone and other birders confirm the ID. BirdMap is a citizen science app + live map for bird sightings. Photos and calls are identified on your phone (Google's AIY Birds for photos, Cornell's BirdNET for sound), then other birders confirm or correct the ID. The iOS app is coming soon; the live map works in any browser. Could I show it at a meeting or send you a link to try? Any feedback would help.
3. Follow up once, politely.

**Done when:** you've contacted at least three groups.
