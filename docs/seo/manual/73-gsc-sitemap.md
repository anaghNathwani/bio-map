# Submit the sitemap in Search Console

**Why it helps:** Tells Google every URL you want crawled (16 today, growing automatically as you add pages and posts).

**Who does it:** you (needs your account or your judgement; it can't be done from the repo).

## Steps

1. Search Console → **Sitemaps** (left menu).
2. Under *Add a new sitemap* type `sitemap.xml` and press **Submit**.
3. Status should turn to *Success*. If it says *Couldn't fetch*, wait a few minutes, confirm the URL loads in a browser, and retry.

**Done when:** the sitemap shows *Success* and a discovered-URL count.
