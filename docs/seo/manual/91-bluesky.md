# Bluesky

**Why it helps:** Bluesky has a big, friendly birding community and starter packs of bird accounts.

**Who does it:** you (needs your account or your judgement; it can't be done from the repo).

## Steps

1. Create an account for the project (or use your own).
2. Post with a screenshot of the map and alt text. Draft: `Made a citizen science bird app: photos and calls get identified on your phone, then other birders confirm the ID. Live map works in your browser: https://birdmap-citizenscience.web.app/map #birding #birdsofbluesky`.
3. Follow and interact with birders first; posting only links gets little reach.

**Done when:** the post is up.
