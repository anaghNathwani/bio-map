# Hashnode cross-post

**Why it helps:** Same idea as Dev.to on another audience.

**Who does it:** you (needs your account or your judgement; it can't be done from the repo).

## Steps

1. In Hashnode's editor, open *Article settings* → *Are you republishing?* and enter the original post URL.
2. Use the post `/blog/keeping-bird-id-on-your-phone`.

**Done when:** the cross-post is live with the original URL set.
