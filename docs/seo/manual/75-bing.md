# Bing Webmaster Tools

**Why it helps:** Bing (and search engines that use its index) is free extra reach, and the setup is one click after Search Console.

**Who does it:** you (needs your account or your judgement; it can't be done from the repo).

## Steps

1. Go to https://www.bing.com/webmasters and sign in.
2. Choose **Import from Google Search Console** and authorise it; your verified property and sitemap come across.
3. Check **Sitemaps** lists `sitemap.xml`.

**Done when:** the site shows as verified in Bing Webmaster Tools.
