# Free press release and local media

**Why it helps:** Coverage in a news site or university paper is a real backlink.

**Who does it:** you (needs your account or your judgement; it can't be done from the repo).

## Steps

1. Free wire sites (PRLog, OpenPR) are easy but their links are mostly nofollow, so treat them as low value.
2. Better: pitch a campus newspaper, local news 'community' section, or a birding blog with the angle "student-built app identifies birds offline" (if that's true for you).
3. Keep the pitch to five sentences plus the link and a screenshot.

**Done when:** you've sent at least three pitches.
