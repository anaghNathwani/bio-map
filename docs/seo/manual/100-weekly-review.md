# Weekly Search Console review and a 90-day check

**Why it helps:** SEO for a new site is slow feedback. A short weekly routine tells you what to fix.

**Who does it:** you (needs your account or your judgement; it can't be done from the repo).

## Steps

1. Each week (10 minutes): **Pages** (any *Not indexed* reasons?), **Performance** (which queries show impressions?), **Sitemaps** (still *Success*?).
2. Fix errors first. A new site typically takes weeks to be indexed and a couple of months to see steady impressions; that is normal.
3. At 90 days: list pages with impressions but low clicks and rewrite their titles/descriptions in `web/src/site.js`; list pages not indexed and add internal links to them.
4. Keep publishing and keep earning real links (playbooks 81-98).

**Done when:** you've done the routine for four weeks in a row.
