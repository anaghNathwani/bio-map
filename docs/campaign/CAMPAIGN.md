# "Look up."

A brand campaign for BirdMap in the spirit of a quiet, black-and-white tribute film: it isn't about the product. It's about a kind of
person: the one who stops mid-sentence because something chirped, who looks up when everyone else is looking down.

**The idea:** noticing is a small act, and noticing together changes what we can see.
**The line:** *Look up.*
**The sign-off:** the small bird mark and the name BIRDMAP. Nothing else. No app, no features, no availability.

This is original writing and imagery. It borrows the *spirit* of that famous 1990s campaign (a montage tribute, a hushed voice, a
one-line ending) but none of its words, footage or tagline.

## How it's made

- **Picture:** a murmuration, a seeded simulation of ~3,000 birds, rendered in pure black and white. No stock footage, no photos: nothing to license.
- **Type:** Fraunces (the brand serif), white on black, one line at a time, fading in and out slowly.
- **Score:** an original slow felt-piano figure over a warm pad, one swell into "You're the one who noticed.", one hanging note at the end. Synthesized, so also license-free.
- **Voice:** none in the files. See below.

**Casting a voice:** a real person beats any synthetic voice here. Someone warm and unhurried, not an announcer: a birder, a teacher,
a grandparent. Record in a quiet room on a phone held 15 cm away, then send me the file and I'll mix it under the score.

## Rules for the campaign

1. **The thought only.** No mention of the app, its features, or "coming soon". The film sells a way of looking, not a download.
2. **Black and white.** No clay orange, no colour. The only warm thing is the voice.
3. **One line at a time.** If a line needs a second sentence to explain it, cut it.
4. **Don't borrow.** No text, tagline or footage from anyone else's campaign, and no real people's faces or quotes.
5. **Claims stay poetic.** Nothing that reads as a statistic ("millions of birds"), a ranking or a guarantee.
